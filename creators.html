Press facts

Ashley Claudy writes New Adult romance about people who live a little too fast and love a little too hard: street racers, fighters, football players, and the women who can hold their own against all of them.

She's a mom, a wife, and a teacher. A perpetual learner fueled by coffee and a wild imagination, a proud University of Maryland alum, a late-night book junkie and a daytime dreamer.
